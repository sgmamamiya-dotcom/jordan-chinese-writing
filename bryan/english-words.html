Banish Boring Words

Swap tired words for strong, exact ones and your compositions come alive.

😴 The soccer player moved fast.

💡 The soccer player streaked past the defenders and sprinted toward the goal.

How to use the book

  1. One list a week. Don't read the whole book. Pick one page (e.g. "Said" words) and practise only that.
  2. Star 5 words you really like from that list. Learn what each one means — "trudged" and "strolled" are both slow, but feel very different.
  3. Before writing, keep the book open next to your plan. Choose 2–3 starred words for your story.
  4. After writing, circle every said, went, ran, ate, very, nice and upgrade at least three of them.
  5. Don't overdo it. One great word per sentence is enough. A sentence stuffed with fancy words sounds strange.
